On the persistence of tables

Every page you are looking at is built out of nested tables, because that is how it was done. The outer one fixes the width at 560 pixels, the inner ones make the columns, and cellspacing="0" closes the seams so the whole thing reads as one surface rather than a grid.

Inline images sit in the run of text, floats push the paragraph aside, and a stray bold or italic span shares its line with everything around it.

What this page uses

  • A tiled background, and a white table on top of it
  • Presentational attributes throughout — bgcolor, align, valign, and <font>
  • An ordered list, for good measure:
    1. nested inside an unordered one
    2. with its own numbering
